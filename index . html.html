Work

Bandgap voltage reference

Analog IC design

A temperature-independent reference producing approximately 1.2 V, for voltage regulators, ADCs, DACs and power management ICs. The design sums a CTAT voltage from a diode-connected BJT with a PTAT voltage generated by two transistors at different emitter areas, weighted by a resistor ratio so the opposing temperature coefficients cancel. Current mirrors hold the branch currents in proportion and reduce supply sensitivity.

Published paper, co-authored with Bojja Ravi Chandra Prasad, under the guidance of Mr. K. Maniraj, MLRIT.

Python, Flask
